# Open conjectures and questions

**All entries here are unproven or unresolved.**

## CONJ-0001 (open research question, not yet a precise conjecture)

Under what geometric and sampling assumptions does a disconnected radius-graph embedding with calibrated cross-component constraints yield lower true geodesic distortion than a tuned globally connected neighborhood-graph embedding?

**Falsification route:** sample folded and near-self-intersecting manifolds. Euclidean cross-component distances may force incorrect proximity, providing straightforward counterexamples to universal claims.

## CONJ-0002 (open research question)

Can we relate `r`, local sampling density, graph component structure and the optimal within/between weight `alpha` without tuning on evaluation geodesic labels?

**Potential failure:** radius-graph connectivity need not predict geodesic distortion in curved manifolds.
