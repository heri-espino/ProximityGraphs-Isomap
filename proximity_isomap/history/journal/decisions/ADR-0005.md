# ADR-0005 — Adopt separation–distortion as the working paper thesis

**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Status:** Accepted as a research direction; not a validated novelty claim.

## Context

Earlier exploratory local experiments EXP-0002–EXP-0008 compared hybrid stress, componentwise Isomap and increasingly many Swiss rolls. They motivated an explicit goal: different graph components should remain geometrically distinct in the final embedding, rather than merely lack graph edges. Their full source and frozen results are not on current GitHub main.

## Options considered

1. Present another disconnected-Isomap algorithm focused only on good visualizations.
2. Use silhouette or within-cluster inertia as the single optimization target.
3. Formulate a controlled intrinsic-preservation, separation and cross-component-placement problem, with an interpretable separation–distortion frontier.

## Decision

Pursue (3) as the **working central thesis**. For the first method, independently embed graph components, retain their computed internal geometry under rigid transformations, and optimize their relative placement subject to a positive geometric separation margin. Track all three evaluation axes separately; use silhouette only as supplementary descriptive evidence.

## Why

Inertia does not change under rigid alignment and thus cannot distinguish overlapped from separated components. Silhouette can improve under arbitrary translation while distorting cross-component placement. A hard clearance constraint and cross-stress objective make the tradeoff explicit. This does not imply the formulation is novel.

## Consequences / falsification criteria

- Confirm graph connected components without using true manifold identities; report false splits and merges on synthetic data.
- Benchmark against established multi-manifold methods and standard/tuned Isomap; verify original literature via literature/pdf, markdown and references.
- Show a reproducible frontier with limitations, rather than only selected pleasing projections.
- Preserve EXP-0002–EXP-0008 as **local-only exploratory evidence** until merged with reproducible commits.
- Revisit or reject this thesis if prior work already solves the same constrained objective, or if separation buys no scientifically useful benefit.
