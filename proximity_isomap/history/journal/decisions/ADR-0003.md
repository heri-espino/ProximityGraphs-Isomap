# ADR-0003 — Candidate disconnected embedding methods

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Status:** Proposed (not implemented)

## Alternatives for future comparison

1. **Full hybrid stress:** minimize normalized within-component graph-geodesic stress plus between-component Euclidean stress over all low-dimensional point coordinates.
2. **Rigid component alignment:** embed each component independently and solve for only its translations and orthogonal transformations, preserving internal embedding distances.

Neither is guaranteed to recover correct manifold geodesics; both are closely related to existing MDS/alignment approaches. Compare against PCA/Euclidean MDS and standard Isomap on the same observations, keeping metric choice and parameter selection explicit.
