# ADR-0001 — Research-first monorepo

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Status:** Accepted provisionally

## Decision

Keep installable Python code, formal notes, historical journal, experiment protocols, results and paper in one repository. Maintain a small `context/` layer and root `AGENTS.md` to let collaborators resume with a short reading path.

## Alternatives

- Separate code/paper/Obsidian repositories: more boundaries, but harder to keep source revisions, experiments and proofs synchronized.
- Single notebook-only project: simple, but poor package boundaries and reproducibility.

## Consequences

Keep notes portable as Markdown; use unique IDs and links; avoid repeated copied claims and oversized Git data.
